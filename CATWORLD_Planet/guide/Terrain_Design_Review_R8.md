# Catworld R8: terrain design review

This revision keeps the established world layout. It changes the terrain language within that geography. These are art-directed interpretations of physical processes, not results of an erosion or climate simulation.

## What was making the model look constructed

1. **Northern mountains:** mirrored elevation samples and narrow residual ridges produced repeated bands. The revised relief uses bounded samples of the licensed terrain analogue, a broader regional elevation field and irregular exposed massifs. The northern province remains enormous; valleys interrupt it rather than reducing it to one thin chain.
2. **Steppe and farmland:** the earlier lowlands had repeated lumps and regular colour patches. Lowland relief is now subdued. Moisture, altitude, valley position and smaller tonal variation govern the surface. Southern agricultural districts remain fertile; the upper plateau and northern foothills remain steppe.
3. **Seattle forest country:** the forest is a regional western watershed, with transitional woodland and wetter valleys. Its boundary varies with moisture and terrain. It is not a small isolated tree patch.
4. **Main rivers:** uniform-width lines are replaced by narrower variable-width channels with asymmetric bends. Tributary endpoints move with their parent channels, and the recorded water profiles descend. Dense pixel sampling also checks for gaps; metre-scale steps can remain because the world raster is coarse. The mountain feeder follows the latest creator redline northwest of the reservoir; Seattle remains the western outlet. These channels are exaggerated at planetary scale and are not navigation charts.
5. **Reservoir:** the lake remains a 690 m level surface over a drowned plateau valley. Small tributary coves and uneven shoulders interrupt the perimeter. The western overflow and eastern dam occupy the same established outlet corridors. Dual outlets require the documented level-control premise; this is not a calculated operating scheme.
6. **Small lakes:** three distinct elongated or hooked basins replace matching branching stamps. Each has one water level, a bed below that level and a surrounding transition into local relief. Catchment water balances remain unmodelled.
7. **Impact basin:** an ancient crater should not be a pristine ring. Eroded sectors vary in height and staining. Red water and soil represent iron-rich suspended sediment and weathered oxides; their colour does not by itself prove an ore grade or metallic iron deposit.
8. **Eastern continent:** the approved rotation, collision connection and leeward desert remain. Relief and materials vary through rock, dry basins, foothills and coastal moisture. No new connection to the southern continent is introduced.
9. **Southern mainland and islands:** the closer, more southerly asymmetric mainland is retained. Five islands remain, with larger fragments near the southern mainland and smaller remnants toward Panterra. The shelf is a proposed rifted continental margin with an old arc inheritance; the Aleutian analogy alone cannot justify a land bridge across deep ocean.
10. **Poles:** spherical terrain fields remove the meridian pinching and radial texture artefact. Coastlines are locally softened, snow responds to terrain, and cold exposed ground is rock and tundra. Sea-ice leads are illustrative seasonal texture, not a computed ice forecast.
11. **Waterfall:** irregular caprock lips, a bending channel, near-vertical falls, plunge pools and talus replace straight rectangular water ramps. The local model retains 1,090 m upstream water and 690 m reservoir level with a 250 m main fall plus lower drops. It is a local design study, not a literal extrusion of the low-resolution planetary terrain.
12. **Dam gorge:** the engineered dam remains regular; its surrounding gorge has more varied shoulders. The structural, discharge and energy figures remain explicitly fictional proposals.

## Scale and continuity

- Planet radius 6,100 km; 35 degree tilt; 4,000 Earth-year orbit.
- Northern late autumn and southern late spring are the current working epoch.
- One coherent globe supplies all regional and polar views. Its relief is exaggerated 10 times.
- The 8K colour map has antialiased vector river edges; the elevation, land mask and roughness remain 4K drawing-reference assets. Colour resampling does not create higher-resolution physical terrain. At the equator, one 4,096-pixel map texel spans about 9.4 km. Small settlements, true river widths, individual farms and engineering details require local studies.
- The main and eastern continents remain connected; the southern mainland remains separate at present sea level. The deep eastern/southern ocean remains open.
- The 65 m historic lowstand, migration date, heat source, climate response and detailed tectonic history remain proposals for creator approval.
